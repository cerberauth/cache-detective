## Severity model

Findings are classified critical/high/medium/low/info, following [reportx](https://github.com/cerberauth/reportx)'s severity scale. A finding's severity/CVSS/CWE/OWASP metadata comes from the *check* that raised it — see the "Design notes" section of the [project README](https://github.com/cerberauth/cache-detective#design-notes) for why security-relevant misconfigurations (like an authenticated response marked cacheable) are split into their own check rather than sharing a general-purpose one.
